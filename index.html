Riverkeeper

The river is full of trash and the animals are leaving. Keep it clean so they stay.

  • Steer. Hold and drag on the screen, or use WASD / arrow keys.
  • Scoop. Drive the net at the front of the boat through floating trash.
  • Stop the sea. Trash that floats past the end hurts the river.
  • Look around. Wide, Boat and Wildlife views, or keys 1, 2, 3.
  • Earn coins. Every piece of trash is a coin. Spend them in the Boat shop.
